## What is Bizum?

Bizum is a mobile payment service created in 2016 by a consortium of Spanish banks. It allows you to send and receive money instantly between individuals, directly from your banking app, using only a phone number — with no need to enter a bank account number or IBAN.

Unlike PayPal or Revolut, Bizum is not a standalone app: it is integrated into your bank's app. You don't need to create a separate account or top up a wallet. The service is **free for individuals** and works in real time, 24/7.

Since its launch, Bizum now has more than **29.5 million active users** in Spain, making it the country's dominant mobile payment method.

## Prerequisites: what you need before activating Bizum

To activate Bizum, three conditions are required:

**1. A bank account at a compatible Spanish bank**
This is the only essential requirement. Bizum is directly linked to your bank account — it cannot exist without it. Your bank must be one of the institutions that have integrated the service ([more than 30 banks in Spain](https://bizum.com/es/bancos), see the full list in Section 7).

**2. A mobile phone number**
Bizum identifies users by their phone number. Each number can only be linked to **one Bizum account**.

**3. Your bank's mobile app**
Bizum is activated from your bank's app, not from a website or a standalone Bizum application. Make sure you have the app installed and active access to your online banking.

## How to activate Bizum

Bizum activation is done entirely from your bank's mobile app. The process varies slightly from one bank to another, but the steps are always the same:

1. **Open your bank's app** and log in to your account.
2. **Look for Bizum** in the main menu — usually in the "Transfers", "Payments" section or directly in a dedicated "Bizum" tab.
3. **Accept the terms and conditions** of Bizum.
4. **Enter your mobile phone number** — this is the number that will be linked to your Bizum account.
5. **Validate by SMS** — you will receive a confirmation code to enter in the app.
6. **Choose the bank account** you want to link to Bizum (if you have multiple accounts).

Once these steps are complete, Bizum is active and ready to use.

> **Important:** If your bank does not appear in the list of compatible banks (see Section 7), it does not yet offer Bizum.

## Sending and receiving money

Once Bizum is activated, sending money to someone takes just a few seconds:

1. Open your bank's app and go to Bizum.
2. Select **"Send"**.
3. Enter the recipient's phone number or choose them from your contacts.
4. Enter the amount and add an optional message.
5. Confirm the operation — the money is transferred immediately.

**Receiving money** requires no action: as soon as someone sends you a Bizum transfer, the amount is credited instantly to your account.

You can also **send a payment request**: you specify the amount and the recipient's number, who then receives a notification and can accept or decline.

## Paying at merchants

Bizum is not limited to transfers between individuals — it is also accepted as a payment method at many online and physical merchants in Spain.

**Online:** When making a purchase on a site that accepts Bizum, select "Bizum" as your payment method at checkout. You will be prompted to enter your phone number, then receive a notification in your banking app to confirm the payment.

**In store:** Some physical merchants offer a Bizum QR code at the till. Simply scan it from your bank's app to pay directly.

## Limits, caps and security

Bizum imposes limits per operation and per period, set nationally by Bizum. Some banks apply lower limits.

**Limits between individuals:**

| Operation | Limit |
|---|---|
| Minimum per transaction | €0.50 |
| Maximum per transaction (send/receive) | €1,000 |
| Maximum sent per day | €2,000 |
| Maximum sent per month | €5,000 |
| Number of receipts per month | 60 operations |

*These limits do not apply to merchant payments or donations.*

**Security:**
Bizum uses your bank's security system. Each operation is confirmed through your bank's app (PIN, fingerprint or Face ID as applicable). Bizum does not store your banking details — your bank handles the transaction.

If you receive a payment by mistake, you can return it directly from the app. In case of a dispute, your bank handles the claim.
